# Unlocking Growth: Talent Acquisition Strategy for Clinica dell'Udito

**Course:** Cambiamento Organizzativo e Progettazione dei Processi Aziendali  
**Università di Bologna · LM Ingegneria Gestionale · a.a. 2025/2026**  
**Role:** Leadership, Mediation & Motivation

---

## Overview

Clinica dell'Udito is a fast-growing hearing care network across Bologna, 
Ferrara, and Ravenna, facing a critical bottleneck: a national shortage of 
qualified audiologists is constraining its ability to scale. The project 
applied Design Thinking, following the Stanford d.school and Double 
Diamond models, to move from problem space to a validated, deployable 
solution.

---

## Research & Problem Definition

Through stakeholder interviews, on-site observation, and Galbraith's Star 
Model, three Evidence-Problem-Opportunity (EPO) areas were identified:

1. **Organizational growth constraints** — centralized decision-making and 
   unclear role definition
2. **Audiologist shortage and recruitment difficulty** — a national 
   decline in academic placements (340 → 299 seats between 2021 and 2025) 
   combined with high competition from multinational players
3. **Lack of structured internal/external communication strategy**

After validating priorities with the company, the team focused on **EPO 2**: 
the shortage of audiologists was identified as the most urgent constraint, 
since organizational and communication issues become secondary if the 
business cannot operate at all.

---

## From Hypotheses to Solution

Three business hypotheses were prototyped and tested in the field:

| Hypothesis | Test | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| University outreach (flyers, early internships) | WhatsApp groups, Google Form | Invalidated — reach too limited |
| Relocation support for out-of-region candidates | Cost simulation + owner validation | **Validated — selected** |
| Recruiting from competitor dissatisfaction | Survey + ~100 Glassdoor reviews analyzed | Validated, but deprioritized |

The relocation hypothesis was selected as the strategic priority: it most 
directly expands the talent pool nationally and removes the single biggest 
adoption barrier — relocation risk.

---

## Final Solution

A structured **12-month relocation support package** designed to make 
out-of-region hiring practically viable:

**Phase 1 (months 1–3, trial period)** — temporary B&B accommodation, 
capped at €1,200/month, avoiding long-term lease commitments during 
probation.

**Phase 2 (months 4–12)** — company-leased housing (Uso Foresteria 
contract), removing upfront costs for the new hire while preserving fiscal 
control for the company.

**Phase 3 (month 12+)** — lease transferred to the employee, marking full 
housing autonomy once the critical onboarding phase is complete.

**Estimated cost:** ~€17,200 per hire over the first year.

---

## Validation

**Internal:** the owner approved the investment after reviewing projected 
cash flows.

**External (the project's critical X-function):** 11 out-of-region 
audiologists were contacted directly via LinkedIn. 4 expressed positive 
interest, 3 found the offer compelling but were not ready to relocate 
short-term, confirming real market demand for the proposal.

---

## Economic Modeling

- **NPV analysis** over a 12-month horizon, using a Poisson distribution 
  to model unit sales (mean: 7 devices/month per audiologist)
- **Sensitivity analysis** confirms positive NPV above a threshold of 
  ~70 devices sold/year per hire
- **Risk mitigation:** a 3-year stability pact with financial penalty 
  clause protects the investment against early turnover

---

## System Dynamics Simulation (AnyLogic)

A System Dynamics model was built to simulate the relationship between 
client acquisition, audiologist workload pressure, and hiring velocity.

**Without the relocation bonus:** workload pressure escalates to a 
critical index of 2.5, capping growth at 90 new clients and ~€65,000 in 
annual revenue.

**With the relocation bonus active:** 7 new hires are secured, workload 
pressure stabilizes within 4 months, and the clinic reaches 380 new 
clients and ~€230,000 in annual revenue — a fourfold increase.

---

## KPIs Defined for Monitoring

- **Number of new hires** vs. ideal staffing benchmark
- **Time to Fill** — target: 1–2 months (vs. over a year of unsuccessful 
  search historically)
- **Payback Period** — break-even targeted within the first year per hire
- **Retention Rate** — target: 100% retention at 12–24 months post-bonus
